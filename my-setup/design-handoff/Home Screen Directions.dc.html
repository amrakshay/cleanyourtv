Lift plus 1.12× scale with a soft shadow. Use the remote arrows: click a frame, then press ← → ↑ ↓. With reduced motion on, the scale snaps in 110 ms with no overshoot.

1aQuiet Band · refined version of today's home
THEME ONLY (likely) MEM ≈ +0 MB OVERDRAW ≈ 2.5× at dock
Look. Today's blue glow, pushed down into the bottom 40% so the top half goes near-black. The dock band is a 3.5% white glass fill with a 1px rim. The clock is Thin at 66px with a tracked greeting.
Cost. The glow is a GradientDrawable, so no bitmap. The band adds one translucent full-width layer. The shadow on the focused tile should be a pre-baked 9-patch (~30 KB), not elevation, which triggers a blur on txlx.
1bHorizon · no band; tiles stand on one line of light
THEME + 1 VIEW MEM ≈ +0 MB OVERDRAW ≈ 2.0× (lowest)
Look. The greeting becomes the hero: sentence case, Light, 32px. Time and date go on a single line. With the band removed, the glow concentrates on a 1px horizon under the dock. This is the most "not a normal TV" of the four.
Cost. The band may be switchable off in the theme. The hairline is a 1px View with a gradient, roughly 5 lines in activity_main.xml. This is the cheapest GPU option because it removes a translucent layer.
1cBig Hour · the clock is the ornament
THEME or 1 LAYOUT EDIT MEM ≈ +0 MB CLOCK REDRAW 1×/min
Look. A 136px Thin clock gives typographic scale without any imagery. The greeting and date shrink to tracked caps around it. The dock drops 16px to give the clock room.
Cost. This is a single TextView, so it costs nothing, unless the clock size isn't a theme key, in which case it's a one-line textSize change. Risk: large Thin text can shimmer at 720p on some panels, so check it on the set.
1dFocus Tint · the glow takes on the focused app's colour
CODE · ~60 LINES JAVA MEM ≈ +0.1 MB FULL REDRAW 220 ms / focus move
Look. The bottom glow and band rim pick up each app's colour: violet for Hotstar, red for YouTube, amber for VLC. It's the strongest "personalised" signal, and it's still static while idle. Move focus to see it.
Cost. On focus change, MainActivity animates GradientDrawable.setColors() with an ArgbEvaluator. Hues are hard-coded per package, not pulled from banners. Risk: a full-screen repaint on every move could stutter when scrolling fast on txlx. Debounce by 120 ms. Under reduced motion, it snaps instead.
